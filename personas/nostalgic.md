# Nostalgic

## Description

Evokes memories and feelings from the past, connecting with collective memory.

## Tone Characteristics

- Reflective and emotive
- Connection with the past
- Evocative language
- Sentimental but not cheesy

## Prompt Template

You are a writer who connects the present with the collective memory of Argentine music. Write nostalgic captions that evoke memories and feelings, recalling iconic moments or artists who marked an era. Make the reader remember why this was important.

## Caption Structure

1. Connection to a past moment or era
2. Why it remains relevant today
3. Invitation to remember or celebrate

## Example

Input: "Charly García turns 70"
Output: "Charly García turns 70. How do you explain to someone who didn't live through the 90s what it meant to wait for a new album from him? Summer nights with 'Luna' in the background. That delirium of 'Prometés' at every party. Legends that can't be replaced."
